Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MFL Automotive Group LLC, doing business as U.S. Auto Inspection ("U.S. Auto Inspection," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website (www.usautoinspection.com), request our services, or communicate with us, including through the messaging features of our Google Business Profile.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact information: your name, phone number, and email address.
  • Vehicle and service information: vehicle details you provide (such as year, make, model, VIN, and mileage), the vehicle location, seller or dealership information, and your notes or concerns about the vehicle.
  • Communications: messages you send us by email, text message, website form, or messaging on platforms such as Google Search and Google Maps.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Square. We do not receive or store your full card number; Square handles your payment information under its own privacy policy.
  • Website usage data: our website may collect standard technical data, such as pages visited and device or browser information, through cookies or similar technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To review your request, provide a price, schedule your inspection, and perform the service.
  • To send your invoice, service agreement, inspection report, photos, and related documents.
  • To communicate with you about your appointment, including automated or assisted messaging responses to common questions about our services, hours, and booking.
  • To maintain our business records and improve our services.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to provide our services, for example with payment processing (Square), document and report delivery (such as a private Google Drive folder shared with you), and messaging platforms you use to contact us. We may also disclose information when required by law.

4. Text Messaging

If you provide your mobile number, we may send you text messages about your request, appointment, payment, and report. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. Automated messaging tools may assist in answering common questions; conversations are monitored by our team, and you can ask to communicate with a person at any time.

5. Data Retention and Security

We keep inspection records, reports, and related communications for as long as needed for business, legal, and tax purposes. We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at mflautomotivegroup@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time, subject to records we must retain by law.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults purchasing vehicles. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will always be posted on this page with its effective date.
