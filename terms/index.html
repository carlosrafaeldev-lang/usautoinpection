1. Our Services

We provide used vehicle pre-purchase inspections at the location of the vehicle (dealership, private seller, or other agreed location) throughout Southwest Florida, as well as vehicle history review services and digital educational products. An inspection includes a visual, non-invasive review of the vehicle's condition, photo documentation, an OBD-II diagnostic scan when vehicle communication is available, and a road test when the seller or dealer permits it and the vehicle is safe and legal to drive. A digital report is delivered after the inspection.

Your inspection is officially booked and your appointment time is reserved only after the invoice is paid. A signed service agreement may also be required before the inspection is performed.

If the vehicle is sold before an inspector is dispatched, we refund your payment to the original payment method, minus the Square processing fee. Once an inspector has been dispatched, no refund is issued.

  • Diagnostic scan results depend on OBD access, battery condition, module communication, and scanner compatibility. Some vehicles restrict aftermarket scanner access, and full data cannot be guaranteed.
  • The report reflects the vehicle's condition at the time of inspection only. It is not a warranty, guarantee, or prediction of future condition or reliability, and it is not a state inspection, emissions test, or legal certification.
  • Findings are provided to help you make a purchasing decision. The final decision to purchase any vehicle is yours alone.
