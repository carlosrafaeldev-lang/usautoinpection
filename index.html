What our inspection includes

What We Check During the Inspection

We inspect beyond the listing photos and sales description, helping buyers understand the vehicle’s real condition before making a final decision.

01

Exterior & Body Condition

Paint, dents, rust, glass, lights, trim, panels, visible previous repair signs, and body/frame concerns when visible.

Visual inspection only. No disassembly or body shop measurements.

02

Interior Features & Electronics

Seats, dashboard, climate control, infotainment, windows, locks, lights, safety features, and power functions.

Some features may require special tools, module communication, or seller permission.

03

Engine Bay Review

Visible leaks, hoses, belts, fluids, battery, mounts, cooling system, noises, and visible maintenance concerns.

Engine covers and restricted access may limit visibility. No disassembly.

04

Undercarriage Review

Suspension, steering, exhaust, frame areas, rust, leaks, driveline, and structural condition when accessible.

Lift access depends on seller or dealership. Without a lift, visibility is limited.

05

Tires, Brakes & Suspension

Tire condition, tread wear, visible brake pads, rotors, brake fluid condition, steering, suspension wear, and uneven wear.

Wheels are not removed. Hidden brake components may not be visible.

06

OBD-II Diagnostic Scan

Diagnostic scan report for active, pending, and stored trouble codes when communication is available.

Scan depends on OBD access, battery condition, module communication, and scanner compatibility.

07

Road Test When Permitted

Steering, braking, shifting, acceleration, vibrations, noises, and driving characteristics when seller/dealer allows it.

Road test only when the vehicle is safe and legal to drive.

08

Photo Documentation

Photos of the vehicle condition, important findings, visible concerns, undercarriage access, tires, body, interior, and key details.

Photo quantity varies based on access, lighting, weather, and inspection conditions.

09

Buyer-Friendly Report

Condition notes, important findings, photos, diagnostic results when available, and plain-English explanations for buying decisions.

Report reflects vehicle condition at the time of inspection only.

Clear expectations

What’s Not Included

Our inspection is detailed, but it is a non-invasive visual inspection. These are the main limitations clients should understand before booking.

  • ×No disassembly: we do not remove parts, covers, wheels, panels, spark plugs, or interior trim.
  • ×No state inspection or emissions testing: this is not a legal certification, state inspection sticker, or emissions test.
  • ×No warranty or future guarantee: the report reflects the vehicle condition at the time of inspection only.
  • ×Road test depends on permission: road test is performed only when the seller/dealer allows it and the vehicle is safe and legal to drive.
  • ×Diagnostic scan depends on communication: results depend on OBD access, battery condition, module communication, and scanner compatibility.
  • ×Lift access is not guaranteed: undercarriage inspection depends on safe access or a lift provided by the seller/dealer.

Used car inspection FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to pay before you review my inspection request?
No. You can submit your request first. We review the vehicle details, location, availability, inspection price, and available appointment time before sending a secure Square invoice link to your email and phone. If you agree with the price, terms, and appointment time, you can confirm the inspection by paying the invoice. Your inspection is officially booked only after payment is received.
How fast can you inspect the vehicle?
Same-day and next-day used car inspections may be available in Fort Myers, Cape Coral, and nearby areas depending on schedule, vehicle location, seller access, and payment confirmation.
What does the inspection include?
Our mobile pre-purchase used car inspections typically include exterior, interior, engine bay, visible fluid condition, brakes, suspension, tires, undercarriage access, diagnostic scan, road test notes, detailed photos, and a digital multi-point inspection report. Diagnostic scan, road test, and undercarriage visibility remain subject to vehicle access, seller approval, safe conditions, and scanner communication.
Do you perform a diagnostic scan?
Yes, when the vehicle’s OBD port is accessible and communication is available. Results depend on OBD access, scanner compatibility, battery condition, and module communication.
Do you test drive the vehicle?
Yes, when the seller or dealership permits it and the vehicle is safe and legal to drive. If a road test is not permitted, it will be noted in the report.
Do you inspect the undercarriage?
Yes, when safely accessible. Lift access depends on the dealership or inspection location. If lift access is not available, the undercarriage review is limited to safely accessible visible areas.
Can you inspect a vehicle for an out-of-state buyer?
Yes. Many customers are out-of-state buyers who found a vehicle online in Southwest Florida. We visit the seller or dealer, inspect the vehicle, and send photos and a digital report.
Can I use the report to negotiate?
Yes. Many buyers use inspection findings, photos, diagnostic results, tire/brake observations, leaks, warning lights, and visible repair concerns to negotiate price or decide whether to move forward.
Do you perform state inspections or emissions testing?
No. U.S. Auto Inspection provides pre-purchase used vehicle inspections only. We do not perform state inspections, emissions testing, or inspection stickers.
Is this inspection a warranty or guarantee?
No. This is a non-invasive visual inspection based on the vehicle’s condition at the time of inspection. It is not a warranty, guarantee, certification, appraisal, or promise of future performance.
How do I confirm the inspection?
After you submit your booking request, we review the vehicle information, location, and availability. Then we send a secure Square invoice link to your email and phone. The inspection is officially booked only after the invoice is paid.
