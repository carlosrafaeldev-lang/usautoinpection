Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I get a pre-purchase inspection?
Used cars can hide issues that aren’t visible during a test drive. A pre-purchase inspection can reveal mechanical, electrical, or structural problems, giving you peace of mind and potentially saving you thousands.
What does the inspection include?
Our inspections typically cover:
  • Engine and transmission
  • Brakes and suspension
  • Electrical systems
  • Body and frame condition
  • Tires and wheels
  • Interior features
  • Diagnostic scan (OBD2)
  • Road test (if permitted)
  • Free CarFax Vehicle History Report
  • High-resolution photos of key findings
How long does the inspection take?
Most inspections take 1 to 2 hours, depending on the vehicle's condition and complexity.
What is a pre-purchase car inspection?
A pre-purchase inspection is a comprehensive assessment of a used vehicle’s condition by a professional mechanic before you buy it. It helps you avoid costly surprises and make a confident purchase decision.
Where do you perform the inspection?
We proudly serve the Cape Coral and Fort Myers area. Inspections are performed on-site—whether the vehicle is at a dealership, with a private seller, or at your home.
Can you inspect any type of vehicle?
Yes, we inspect all major makes and models, including sedans, SUVs, trucks, and hybrids. For specialty or exotic vehicles, please contact us first.
Will I get a report?
Absolutely. You'll receive detailed digital reports (in PDF and online formats) covering all aspects of the inspection—plus your free CarFax history report and high-quality photos.
What if the seller doesn’t agree to an inspection?
That’s a red flag. If a seller refuses an inspection, it may be best to walk away from the deal.
How much does it cost?
Our pricing varies depending on the vehicle type and location. Check our services or contact us directly for a quote.
Can I book online?
Yes! Booking is easy—just use our online form or give us a call to schedule an inspection at your convenience.
Do you offer same-day inspections?
In most cases, yes. Availability can vary by location, so contact us to check for urgent requests.
What’s your cancellation policy?
We offer free cancellation or rescheduling with at least 24 hours’ notice. Last-minute cancellations may incur a fee.
